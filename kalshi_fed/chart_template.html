From contracts to a rate

Each contract pays $1 if the upper bound is above strike k. Its price is P(rate > k). Differencing across the ladder gives the probability of each 25bp outcome, and the expected upper bound is the probability-weighted sum.

Pricing a contract each day

Close bid/ask midpoint when the spread is 20¢ or less. Up to 40¢, the last trade clipped to the quote. Wider than that, the strike is dropped. Quotes carry forward on days with no candle. A weighted isotonic fit (tighter quotes weigh more) makes probabilities non-increasing in strike.

Reliability

A meeting-day is flagged unreliable when more than 10% of probability sits beyond the usable strikes (as in 2022, when hikes outran the ladder) or in gaps wider than 50bp between them. Those days are shaded here and left out of the history lines.
